## Behavior and evidence

Updates `river_crossing.json`, `highlands.json` and `enclosed_valleys.json`
in place and removes the duplicate `_calibrated.json` presets. All current
selections keep mating radius **two**; the initial river radius-three selection
was withdrawn. River/valleys lower living costs and predator attack costs further;
highlands increases initial energy within existing caps. All retain reduced
movement costs. Health, starvation damage, maturity, cooldowns, birth costs,
rewards, model signatures and physical mating checks remain unchanged.
Highlands also gives newborns its higher initial energy, an explicit energy-grant
tradeoff rather than evidence of improved learning.

The [calibration guide](../calibration.md) records all stages: the historical
20-run assessment, first six-episode check, 45-episode terrain matrix, and final
proximal-only study. The [harness](../../work/calibrate_terrain.py) supports lower
breeding requirements/costs, higher reserves and lower maintenance costs with
fresh matched seeds and tqdm. Defaults exclude radius expansion; the legacy
radius variant remains explicit for historical reproduction. Only scalar metrics
and captured configurations are written to ignored results. No dependencies,
replays, checkpoints or model archives were added by the experiments.

## Validation and current decision

- 48 additional proximal-only episodes, 1,000 steps each: 27 screening episodes
  on seeds 100–102 and 21 new-seed comparisons on 200–202. Nine exactly matched
  older energy controls were reused for screening; no duplicate control runs.
- All episodes keep radius two. Population/birth/death ledgers balance; initial
  population plus total births stayed below caps, ruling out cap constraints.
- Selected settings versus radius-two energy references on the nine matched
  new-seed episodes: prey births 14 → 34; predator births 7 → 14; predator
  offspring alive at the horizon 3 → 8; starvation deaths 202 → 130.
- River selects lower maintenance costs (predator offspring 1 → 4); highlands
  selects higher reserves (1 → 3); valleys select lower maintenance costs
  (offspring unchanged at one, predator survivors 5 → 10).
- Maintenance alone improved highlands founder survival but worsened predator
  reproduction on new seeds; it was not selected there. Lower birth requirements
  were tested and not adopted. Mature life stages/cooldowns remain intact.
- These comparisons informed configuration selection and are short training
  trials, not an unbiased held-out evaluation, stable coexistence demonstration
  or proof of learning. Highland gains include extra founder/newborn energy.
- All 20 remaining presets validate. Selected parameters match tested candidates and their
  model signatures match originals. Source isolation, proximal invariants,
  ignored outputs, report arithmetic, documentation links and whitespace checked.
- All 19 geography tests pass after updating the original files. A two-episode
  CLI smoke verifies captured baseline loading through `--reference-report`.
  Runtime engine code is unchanged; no broad unit-suite rerun was necessary.

## Adoption and rollback

Use the existing terrain JSON path in a new invocation. Compatible weights can
load because model/action signatures match, but use fresh policies for controlled
comparisons. For the earlier constants, recover the captured baseline configuration from the
original experiment report or Git history. The harness accepts `--reference-report`
for historical comparisons without modifying active presets. No data migration,
viewer change or new dependency is required.
