## Overview

The ordinary-PPO preset now exposes consent, escape and multi-target
observations with feeding and breeding budgets intended for fresh ordinary-PPO
brains. It keeps the unmasked PPO objective and action selection. The separate
masked-PPO recommendation remains scoped to that controller.

## Changes

- Use a 32×24 flat world with 64 prey and six predators; retain prey health 40,
  sight 10 and movement cost 0.1. Mating radii stay proximal: prey 2, predator 3.
- Set predator attack damage 40, attack cost 0.25 and kill energy 80; breeding
  requires 30 energy and costs 10 per parent. Health gates and maturity stay intact.
- Add prey escape actions and five-step mating consent. Both species use
  failed-action reward −0.01, offspring reward +3 and entropy coefficient 0.03.
  Preserve other PPO optimizer settings and rare birth mutation.
- Use version-4 `prey_ppo` (k=3, 69 inputs, nine actions, hidden [16]) and
  `predator_ppo` (k=10, 153 inputs, six actions, hidden [64,32]). Both select lifespan.
- Make bounded recovery/best-brain retention explicit and opt this preset into
  full-detail capture of its final two scheduled episodes. Global default remains all.
- Keep numerical regression fixtures on explicit legacy 20-input contracts and
  fixed entropy settings. Update tutorial comparisons to clear inherited capture
  windows without changing source banks or physical configuration.

## Compatibility and migration

Legacy stock PPO brains do not match these observation/action/network signatures.
Start fresh or load compatible ordinary-PPO banks. Exact resumes and old replays
continue using captured configurations; other algorithm/terrain presets are untouched.
The new selector makes this a current-source preset, not the bundled PyPI 0.0.2.9
preset. No distribution-version bump, publication or historical result rewrite.

## Validation

Configuration and founder observation/brain dimension checks pass. Regression
coverage checks ordinary-PPO dispatch, energy replenishment from an aligned hunt
and parents retaining health/energy after a successful birth. Full-horizon
matched-seed and chained-episode checks inform the current calibration; the actual
CLI is also checked across three 1,500-step episodes. See the
[calibration guide](../calibration.md#ordinary-ppo-adaptation) for the supported
objective and limits. Short execution checks alone do not validate ecological stability.

## Rollout and rollback

Run `python -m wtf run ppo` with current source. Default five-episode runs record
4–5; `--record-last-episodes -1` retains all future playback. Override `--steps`
for short checks, remembering 200 steps is below prey maturity at 300. Retain an
old captured environment with its compatible banks to reproduce older controls.
