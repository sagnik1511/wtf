# Template and figure provenance

This is an author-prepared Nature Machine Intelligence Article manuscript using
standard LaTeX `article.cls`. It follows the journal's
[content-type structure](https://www.nature.com/natmachintell/content) and
[accepted-manuscript guidance](https://www.nature.com/natmachintell/submission-guidelines/aip-and-formatting),
checked 19 November 2026. It is not an official journal-specific class or the
publisher's production layout. No publisher logos or branding are included.

Standard distribution packages include natbib, lineno, geometry, TikZ/PGFPlots,
amsmath, graphicx, booktabs, tabularx, listings and hyperref. They and the
`unsrtnat` numerical bibliography style are provided by the user's TeX
distribution and retain their respective upstream licenses; they are not
vendored here. The earlier TMLR template files are no longer used or bundled.
No manuscript/project license is inferred from these packages.

Figures are repository-specific vector diagrams and data plots. Structural
figures adapt the implementation relationships documented in the repository;
no external artwork is reproduced. The two CSVs transcribe historical values
from the reports named in the supplementary evidence inventory. Schematics are
labelled as such and do not depict measured ecological outcomes.
