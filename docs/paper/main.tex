\begin{abstract}
Learning in a changing population is difficult to separate from favorable
initial conditions and inherited behavior. Wildlife Trial Framework is a
configurable ecosystem simulator with independent reinforcement-learning
controllers, resource competition, predation and terrain-dependent movement.
Eligible pairs choose reproduction; offspring mix parental parameters, may
receive rare mutations and begin with fresh training state. Nine controller
variants use a common observation interface. Headless Python simulation writes
lossless binary replay chunks for a separate JavaScript viewer, while full
checkpoints preserve learning and random-generator state. Historical local
measurements show matched-state native acceleration and a 92.8\% storage
reduction for one model archive. These measurements establish specific
engineering properties, rather than ecological learning or biological validity.
We provide a controlled evaluation protocol and an editable supplementary
observations section for future studies of survival, hunting, inheritance and
generalization.
\end{abstract}
