Species trends over time

Load a recording.

Each point is one recorded simulation step; cards show the current step. Choose a metric for each chart. Missing steps and absent diagnostics are not filled in. Hunting/feeding success is kills per attack for predators and productive feeds per eat attempt for prey; no attempt = no value. Reward excludes animals that died that step. Action coverage reports how many previous animals' actions were recovered.

Groups are same-species proximity clusters: linked animals within 3 cells, including transitive links. Solitary animals are separate. Obstacles are ignored; proximity does not establish cooperation. Prediction dots average sampled agents on their changing observations, not prediction accuracy or proof of learning. Policy probabilities and Q values have separate chart options.

Brain explanation

Archived model analysis — this is not the selected animal’s historical weights or a reconstruction of its thoughts. The archive may come from a later step or another animal. Recorded predictions are shown separately. Following replay changes observations, not the archived weights.

Advanced · What if one observation input changes?

Change one normalized input only. Other inputs and recorded action masks stay fixed; terrain or mating eligibility is not recalculated. Applying a what-if pins this observation and pauses replay. Enable Follow replay samples to return to the playhead.
